Meet Rev. Jade Lee, MDiv

I help people and

Jade Lee is a pastor, PhD scholar, mother, and social entrepreneur committed to cultivating transformative leadership, advancing justice, and empowering voices across nations.

As a humanitarian, I believe in empowering individuals, people groups, and nations to take control of their lives and make positive changes. I work with individuals from all walks of life, and I am committed to providing a safe and non-judgmental space for you or your organization to explore how you can go to the next level. Let's work together to create a better world!

Even if you have never run before, Coach Lee will help you take back your health starting from scratch.

Original Fine Art • Art by Jade

Art for the Soul

In 2020, while pregnant with her youngest son, Corey, Jade began making chalk art with her twin toddlers and soon found a love for pastel painting. Jade's art provides representation for marginalized groups and people of color — Art that Inspires, Encourages, and Transforms the Human Soul.
