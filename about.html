The Full Story

Transforming Lives From the Womb to the Tomb

In 2006, Rev Jade Lee moved with her husband from Hampton, VA to Atlanta metro, GA to minister to runaway youth in the urban core of ATL. During that time she developed a heart for the marginalized, disadvantaged and poor of the world and she gave her life to leading short term mission trips by youth, for youth.

But this servanthood calling really began when as a small child her father brought her with him to feed the homeless of downtown Philly. It was there in a homeless shelter that she led her first convert to Christ, a family of three homeless kids. The rest is history!

Rev Jade Lee has pastored, planted churches around the world, preached at various denominations, cared for the orphan and widow, and shared the gospel from distinguished seminaries to debt bondage slave kilns in unreached areas.

"Always remember, you have within you the strength, the patience, and the passion to reach for the stars, to change the world."
