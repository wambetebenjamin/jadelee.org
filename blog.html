Kendrick Lamar Super Bowl Lament & Moving Towards Healing

Feb 10, 2025 • By Rev. Jade Lee • 6 min read

Kendrick’s work yesterday reminded me of his collab performance with Beyoncé at the 2016 BET Awards, "Freedom." Lament is threaded throughout his Super Bowl performance — it is layered, revolutionary and meant to be More Than Entertainment. It is a Message to America.

We have been using performances and music as message since the spirituals, gospel music, folk, the blues, and now through hip-hop. What others think is entertainment is our internal freedom language in code. Telling our stories is our heritage, and our heritage is our medicine.
