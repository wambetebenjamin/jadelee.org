Pastel Fine Art • Representation

Art that Inspires, Encourages, & Transforms

In 2020, while pregnant with her youngest son, Corey, Jade began making chalk art with her twin toddlers. She soon found a love for the adult form of chalk art, pastel painting, and began gaining requests for commissioned art. Jade's art provides representation for marginalized groups and people of color. Each piece is available in different products in Jade's art store on Society6.
