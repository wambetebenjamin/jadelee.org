Everything You Need to Know

Answers regarding coaching, running mentorship, theological training cohorts, and global outreaches.

Jade Lee is a pastor, PhD scholar, mother of four, All-American track star, and social entrepreneur committed to cultivating transformative leadership, advancing racial justice, and empowering voices across 41 nations. Over the past 20 years, she has helped individuals and organizations get unstuck.
As an 11-time collegiate record breaker and Junior 800 Meter All-American, Coach Lee provides running and fitness coaching for beginner to intermediate runners. Even if you have never run before, she helps you build healthy habits and stamina starting from scratch ($35/hr), as well as consultation for competitive high school distance athletes.
Convergence Global School of Ministry equips emerging leaders across the seven mountains of culture (arts, media, family, religion, education, business, government). Cohorts meet on Fourth Saturdays with Africa Hours (6:00 am EST / 9:00 am – 7:00 pm) and South Asia Hours (9:00 am – 9:00 pm). Applications can be submitted via convergencsm@gmail.com.
An exclusive missional gathering traveling to the Door of No Return in Ghana, Africa. Grounded in the Akan principle of Sankofa ("Reach back and fetch it"), the tour is designed to explore roots, heal souls, and restore dignity as women enter the past and look towards the future.
