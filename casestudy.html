Documented Research • Reconciliation

Healing America's Racial Wounds Through Truth and Narrative

Did you know Jade wrote a book unpacking hours of documented research on how racial tensions began in America? Go on a journey with Jade to learn about our history, learn to reconcile, acknowledge how our past impacts us today, and begin the healing journey.

By examining the origins of division and the deep historical pains of the black community through scriptural and historical lenses, this research bridges divides where emotions can otherwise bankrupt words.

Framework for Healing

The Challenge & The Solution

Transforming cultural hostility into miraculous racial reconciliation through faith and storytelling.

The Challenge

Racial tensions and unresolved trauma that have plagued communities for generations. Historical misrepresentation, post-traumatic slave syndrome, and polarization create defensiveness and grief that prevent genuine unity.

The Solution

A 6-step biblical and historical roadmap: 1. Know Your History, 2. Learn to Reconcile, 3. Acknowledge Injustice, 4. Care for the Marginalized, 5. Heal from the Past, and 6. Share Your Knowledge. Our stories are our heritage, and our heritage is our medicine.
