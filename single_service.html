Elite Athletic Coaching & Training

Dedicated Running Coach for Distance Runners & Sprinters

Jade Lee brings a lifetime of championship-tested running discipline to athletes of all levels. From high school hopefuls striving for state podiums to adult beginners looking to complete their first 5K, Coach Lee designs personalized, injury-preventing training programs.

A 1998 Deptford High School Hall of Fame Inductee and New Jersey State 800m Champion, Jade was an 11-time school record breaker at Hampton University and an NCAA Junior 800m All-American. She also served as distance coach at Shiloh High School in Snellville, GA (2023-2024).

Rate: $35.00 / hour — Custom packages for 4, 8, and 12-week cycles available

Running Programs We Offer

Structured, data-informed coaching tailored to your individual goals and athletic baseline.

High School & Collegiate Athletes

Periodized training for mid-distance (400m, 800m, 1600m), pacing strategy, race tactics, and college track recruitment readiness.

Beginner & 5K / 10K Runners

Safe build-up protocols, proper running gait analysis, cadence optimization, and aerobic base construction without burnout or shin splints.

Intermediate Half & Full Marathoners

Lactate threshold tempo runs, long run pacing, fueling strategies, and taper schedules for setting personal records.

Specialized & Cross Training

Functional strength conditioning, mobility drills, mental resilience coaching, and customized post-injury re-entry plans.
