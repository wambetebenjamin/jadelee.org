# JadeLee.org &mdash; Redesigned with Monoline Template

This repository contains the complete website redesign for **Rev. Jade Lee, MDiv** (Pastor, PhD Scholar at Fuller Theological Seminary, Mother, and Social Entrepreneur), rebuilt using the **Monoline** responsive design template.

Every section, image, biography, service tier, book reference, testimonial, and artwork is **100% authentic content sourced directly from `JadeLee.org` and its original Wix predecessor** (`naveplatinum.wixsite.com/jadeleeorg2`). No placeholder lorem ipsum or AI-generated filler has been added.

---

## 🎨 Three Homepage Design Variations

The redesign includes all 3 distinct design layouts provided by the Monoline template, with a persistent switcher bar at the top of each page for easy previewing:

1. **Design 1: Classic Hero (`index.html`)**
   - High-impact hero section showcasing Jade Lee's core mission: *"Connecting Voices. Empowering Nations."*
   - Direct CTAs to explore her coaching journey and published book *The Ferguson Dilemma*.
   - Feature counter bar (41 Nations, 10 Global Conferences, 5 Ministry Bases, 4 Published Books).
   - Core ministries overview, running coach showcase, book feature, testimonials carousel, and consultation booking.

2. **Design 2: Interactive Carousel Slider (`index_two.html`)**
   - Dynamic multi-slide hero featuring:
     - *Slide 1:* Connecting Voices. Empowering Nations.
     - *Slide 2:* Athletic Excellence &mdash; Coach Me, Lee Running.
     - *Slide 3:* Art for the Soul &mdash; Original Pastel Collections.
   - Smooth animated transitions, full feature grids, and complete ministry portfolio.

3. **Design 3: Dynamic Video & Media Showcase (`index_three.html`)**
   - Media-driven showcase header with video popup modal capability.
   - Focus on intercultural research, Fuller Theological Seminary doctoral scholarship, and community transformation.
